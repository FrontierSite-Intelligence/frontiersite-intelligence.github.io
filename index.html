How we think

Grounded in evidence. Designed for decisions.

We combine computational social science, political and institutional analysis, and geospatial methods to help organizations investigate what could affect their projects on the ground.

  • Geospatial contextSite footprints, infrastructure corridors, environmental exposures, and location-specific signals.
  • Political and governance contextLocal institutions, regulatory implementation, land governance, and project delivery conditions.
  • Traceability and uncertaintyDocumented data sources, timestamps, geographical precision, and transparent limitations.
  • Responsible AIAI-assisted synthesis with human review of consequential assessments and respect for data licensing and privacy.

Where we're starting

Focused first on Kenya and East Africa.

Our initial customer discovery and product validation focus on energy and infrastructure project teams, investors, and development finance organizations operating in the region. We are prioritizing one clear, high-value decision workflow before expanding geographically or into additional use cases.
